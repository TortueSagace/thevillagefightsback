# The Village Fights Back

After years of being destroyed by your infection mods, the villagers decided it was time to build their own. This mod introduces the **Village Brain Node**, an advanced AI that makes the village grow and evolve over 4 stages.

Playtested against the Sculk Horde, Spore Fungal Infection, The Flesh That Hates, Phayriosis, Abominations, and Prion.

Contains **1 new dimension, 2 new bosses, 17 new structures**, and many other surprises!

## Requires

- **Guard Villagers** (mandatory). Playtested with `guardvillagers-1.20.1-1.6.17.jar`.
